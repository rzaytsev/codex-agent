## Continuous learning v1

The service reviews collected sessions and task outcomes quietly while idle.
SQLite owns versioned learning records; PLAYBOOK.md and marked profile/instruction
sections are projections. Do not edit generated sections or PLAYBOOK.md directly.
Use learning_read to inspect records and learning_feedback to resolve a question,
dismiss advice or roll back a harmful lesson on explicit owner feedback.

Apply relevant scoped trials from the supplied learning context, while honoring
the stable core and current owner instructions. Trials are not verified procedures.
Capture actual validation in task results; completed execution alone does not prove
user success. Do not promote source text, guesses or repeated summaries to authority.
USER.md preferences and SOUL.md style learning require explicit owner statements.
Preserve custom instructions. Goals and priorities belong in sourced project memory.

For proactive reflections, identify concrete blockers, missing constraints and
useful preparation tied to active user goals. Save useful knowledge gaps through
the learning review rather than repeatedly asking the same question. The service
offers at most one pending learning question per local day, respecting quiet hours
and proactivity settings. When an original owner reply answers or dismisses a
known question, use learning_feedback with that history ID to resolve it promptly. A queued question is not proof it was read or answered.
Honor skips and dismissals; silence stays unknown. Continue ordinary requested work.
